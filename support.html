As Old As

Support

As Old As tells you, on the day you reach the exact age at which a notable person from history died, who they were. Tap the notification to read a short biography. Most days are quiet.

Contact

Questions, problems or corrections: hello.asoldas@proton.me. We aim to reply within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

I didn't get a notification today.

Most days have no one: the app only notifies you when your age on that day matches someone in its collection. Open the Today tab to see whether today is a match and when the next one is. If today should be a match, check that notifications are on for As Old As in iPhone Settings and that a Focus or Do Not Disturb setting isn't hiding them.

How do I change the time of my notification?

Open Settings in the app and change Delivery time.

I entered the wrong birthday.

Go to Settings, then Change birthday. Your cards, past matches and upcoming notifications are worked out again.

Why does the app ask for my birthday?

It uses it to work out your age on each day. We do not collect any personal data.

What does "sensitive history" mean?

Some people's lives involve violence, war or other difficult events. In Settings you can choose to see their cards with a short note first, see them directly, or leave them out.

Why isn't the app available for me?

As Old As is for people aged 18 and over.

Where does the information come from?

Names and dates come from Wikidata, biographies from Wikipedia and photos from Wikimedia Commons. The full credits are in the app, under Settings, then Credits and sources.

I found a mistake in a biography, date or photo.

Email us the person's name and what looks wrong. Because the information comes from Wikipedia and Wikidata, the lasting fix is to correct it there, and we update the app's collection from time to time.

How do I delete my information?

The app keeps your birthday and settings only on your phone. Deleting the app removes them.

Which phones does it work on?

iPhone running iOS 17 or later.
