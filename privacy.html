As Old As

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Who we are

As Old As (“the app”) is published by an independent developer based in the United Kingdom. You can contact us at hello.asoldas@proton.me.

Short version

We do not collect any personal data. The app has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not send your information to us or to anyone else.

What the app stores on your iPhone

To work, the app keeps a small amount of information on your device only:

  • Your birthday, which the app uses to work out your age and the dates of your notifications.
  • Your settings: notification time, how sensitive history is handled, and an optional country.
  • Which people are on your timeline, which the app works out from your birthday.

This information never leaves your phone through the app. If you back up your iPhone (for example with iCloud Backup), Apple may include the app's data in that backup. That is controlled by Apple and your device settings, not by us.

Notifications

Notifications are created on your phone using the information above. Nothing is sent from a server. You can change the time or turn them off in the app or in iPhone Settings.

Content and sources

The names, dates, biographies and photos in the app are bundled inside it, so the app does not download content while you use it. The text comes from Wikipedia and Wikidata, and the photos from Wikimedia Commons, under their open licences. Credits are in the app, under Settings, then Credits and sources.

Links to other websites

Some buttons open web pages, such as the Wikipedia article for a person or the licence for a photo. These pages open in a browser view inside the app. When you open one, that website (for example Wikipedia, run by the Wikimedia Foundation) can receive technical information such as your IP address, as with any website you visit. Their privacy policies apply to those pages. We do not receive that information.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software.

Children

The app is for people aged 18 and over. It asks for a birthday when you start and does not let anyone under 18 continue. If the entered birthday is under 18, it is not saved.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. To remove everything the app stores, delete the app from your iPhone. If you are in the UK or EU and have any question about your data rights, contact us at hello.asoldas@proton.me.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. If the app ever starts collecting data, we will say so here and in the app before it does.
